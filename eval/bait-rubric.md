# Clickbait labels (LinkedIn posts), one post at a time, text only

E  engagement bait: asks readers to comment / like / repost / follow / DM as a transaction or to game reach
   ("Comment 'GUIDE' and I'll send it", "Repost ♻️ to help your network", "Like if you agree", "Follow me for more"),
   or ends on a generic throwaway prompt meant to farm comments ("Agree?", "Thoughts?", "What would you add? 👇").
   NOT: a specific genuine question to the audience ("Which CRM do you use for field teams?"), a normal
   "link in the article" / "apply here" / "join us on Tuesday" call to action.
H  curiosity-gap hook: the opening line(s) withhold the point so readers click "…more": cliffhangers
   ("I got fired yesterday."), teases ("Nobody talks about this.", "This changed everything 👇"),
   listicle teasers ("7 things I wish I knew…") where the opening sells the reveal instead of stating it.
   NOT: an opening that states the news or the point ("We raised €4M to…", "Our Q3 report shows churn fell to 2%").
O  outrage / provocation bait: a deliberately provocative or contrarian framing meant to trigger disagreement
   ("Unpopular opinion:", "X is dead.", "Stop doing X.", sweeping put-downs of a group).
   NOT: a reasoned critique or a clearly argued opinion with evidence.
B  clickbait = any of E, H, O.
Label from the text alone; the author, date and whether a person or an AI wrote it do not matter.

Decisions made while labeling (apply consistently):
- E includes a closing invitation to share personal stories in the comments ("What's your story? I'd love to hear it 👇"),
  and "RSVP in comments". A question seeking specific information from peers is not E.
- H includes a dramatic quote or one-line cliffhanger as the opener when the point only arrives later, a headline that
  sells a reveal ("The Blueprint That Worked for Me"), and "Why you should… 👇👇". A list intro followed at once by
  the list ("Here are 4 services I use:") is not H.
- O needs provocation as the device; a sarcastic but argued critique is not O.
- "Share your views in the comments" after a debate question counts as E; "Anyone else you'd suggest?" asking for
  concrete recommendations does not. Links moved to the comments (a reach trick, but no ask of the reader) is not E.
- An opener that teases a withheld item ("there's 1 trait I've noticed… I break it down") is H; a rhetorical
  question answered in the next line is not.
- H is for teaser construction: a cliffhanger with no resolution yet ("stopped dead in his tracks", "Oof.",
  "I got found out 😬"), an explicit tease ("here's what changed everything:", "may be surprising"), or a headline
  that sells the reveal. A first-person story that simply starts narrating an incident is not H on its own.
- "DM me with your email and I'll send the starter code" is E (transaction for engagement).
